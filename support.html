Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
No. All scanning, compression and conversion happen on your device.

I deleted something by mistake.
Deleted photos and videos go to the "Recently Deleted" album in the Photos app, where you can recover them for up to 30 days.

How do I add the storage widget?
Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit (or +), then Add Widget, and search for Spaciful. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize and add Spaciful.

I forgot my Secret Library PIN.
If Face ID or fingerprint unlock is turned on, use it to open the library and change the PIN from the menu. For your privacy we cannot recover a PIN, because it never leaves your device.

Will I lose my Secret Library if I delete the app?
Yes. Open the Secret Library and choose "Restore all to Photos" before deleting Spaciful.

How do I cancel my subscription?
On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Spaciful. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open the Account tab and tap "Restore purchases", using the same Apple ID or Google account.

How do I delete my account?
Open the Account tab and tap "Delete account", or email us.

Spaciful can't see my photos.
Allow full photo access: Settings → Spaciful → Photos → Full Access.
