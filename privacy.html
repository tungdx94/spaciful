Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Spaciful ("the app", "we") helps you find and remove unneeded photos and videos, compress videos, convert Live Photos to still photos, keep private photos in a locked Secret Library, and see how full your storage is. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Your photos and videos stay on your device

With your permission, Spaciful reads your photo library to find duplicates, similar shots, screenshots, blurry photos and large videos. All analysis, video compression and Live Photo conversion happen locally on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded to our servers or shared with anyone. Items are only deleted when you confirm, and deleted items go to your system's "Recently Deleted" album.

Secret Library

Photos and videos you move to the Secret Library are copied into a private folder inside the app that other apps cannot read. They are never uploaded. Your PIN is stored only as a salted hash in the device Keychain (iOS) or Keystore (Android). Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint unlock are handled entirely by the operating system; Spaciful never receives your biometric data. Items in the Secret Library are included in your device backup but are deleted if you delete the app, so restore them to your photo library first.

Storage widget and app usage (Android)

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets only read your device's total and free storage. On Android, if you grant "Usage access", Spaciful reads the size and last-used date of installed apps to show apps you haven't used for a while. This information stays on your device.

Optional sign-in

You can use the app without an account. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name, email address and a user identifier through Firebase Authentication (Google). This is used only to keep your subscription linked to you across devices.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history.

Data stored on your device

Settings such as your language, video compression level and cleanup history are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

What we don't do

We don't show ads, we don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your data with data brokers.

Third-party services

Firebase Authentication (privacy) and RevenueCat (privacy), only as described above.

Data retention and deletion

Account data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from the Account tab in the app, or by emailing us at kakarevert@gmail.com. We delete your account data within 30 days. Subscriptions must be cancelled separately in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Children

Spaciful is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available on this page.
